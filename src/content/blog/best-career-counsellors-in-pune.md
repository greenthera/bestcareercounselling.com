## 2. Dheya Career Mentors

Dheya Career Mentors is a career mentoring organisation that offers career counselling in Pune, online and in person. It started in 2006 and was founded by Anand Desai. It uses its own psychometric tool, called RAPD, which it says is built for Indian students. The tool matches personality, interests and abilities with more than 22,000 occupations. Dheya also runs programmes with schools in Pune and Pimpri Chinchwad.

**Services Offered:**

- Stream selection and career discovery after Class 10
- Free first consultation
- A 25-minute online psychometric test
- College shortlist and application help for exams like JEE and NEET
- Career guidance for working professionals
- Career programmes for schools

**Best for:** Families who want a mentor-led process with a tested assessment behind it.

**Visit:** [dheya.com](https://dheya.com)
